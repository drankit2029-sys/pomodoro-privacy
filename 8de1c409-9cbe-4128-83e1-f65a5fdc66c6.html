01

ON-DEVICE DATA & LOCAL STORAGE

SHAREDPREFERENCES

What is stored on your phone

To preserve your timer configuration and daily focus progress across app launches, Pomodoro stores lightweight preferences locally in Android's sandboxed SharedPreferences file (pomodoro_timer_state). This data never leaves your device to any server owned by us:

Session Length Preferences

Your custom durations for Focus (5–60 min), Short Break (5–30 min), and Long Break (10–60 min).

Local Only

Today's Rhythm Progress

Completed focus session count, focused minutes, and current epoch day. Automatically resets to 0 at 12:00 AM local time or whenever you tap RESET TODAY.

Auto-Resets Daily

Active Countdown State

Current phase, remaining milliseconds, monotonic elapsed-realtime deadline, and system boot count so running timers recover cleanly if the app is backgrounded.

Ephemeral

Alarm & Ringtone Preferences

Whether the completion alarm and vibration are enabled, ring duration (10s–60s), and the system URI of your chosen alarm tone.

Local Only

Android Cloud Backup & Device-to-Device Transfer

In accordance with standard Android conventions (backup_rules.xml and data_extraction_rules.xml), your local sharedpref settings may be backed up by your Android operating system to your personal Google Drive account or transferred when you set up a new phone. If a custom ringtone URI from an older device cannot be resolved after a transfer, the app automatically falls back to your phone's default alarm sound. We have no access to your Google Drive backups.

02

THIRD-PARTY ADVERTISING & CONSENT

ADMOB & UMP SDK

How native ads and privacy choices work

To keep Pomodoro free to use without subscriptions or paywalls, the app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display a single native sponsored card within the app layout, paired with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK to respect your regional privacy rights.

Consent-First Initialization (EEA, UK & Regulated Regions)

Before any advertisement is requested, Pomodoro queries your consent status via Google's User Messaging Platform (AdsConsentManager). The Google Mobile Ads SDK is only initialized after UMP confirms that consent has been obtained or is not required in your jurisdiction.

DATA COLLECTED BY GOOGLE MOBILE ADS SDK

When ads are active, Google may automatically collect and process the following categories of data on your device to deliver, measure, and prevent fraud in advertisements:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Your resettable Android Advertising ID (AD_ID), app set ID, and basic device parameters (device make, model, operating system version, screen size, and language).
  • IP Address & Approximate Location: Your device's IP address, which may be used by Google to estimate coarse/approximate geographic location for regional ad relevance, regulatory compliance, and security/fraud prevention. (Pomodoro never requests GPS or precise location permissions.)
  • Ad Interactions & Diagnostics: Information about whether an ad was loaded, viewed (impressions), or tapped, as well as ad load error diagnostics (LoadAdError) to maintain stability.

How to change or withdraw your ad consent

1. Inside Pomodoro: Tap the Alarm Settings button (clock icon at the top right) and tap Privacy choices → MANAGE to reopen the Google UMP consent form at any time.
2. In Android Settings: Open Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads) to reset or delete your Advertising ID.

DEVICE PERMISSIONS & SYSTEM APIS

ANDROIDMANIFEST.XML

Why each permission is requested

Pomodoro requests only the minimum Android permissions necessary to keep your countdown accurate in the background, ring your alarm on time, and display the sponsored native ad card:

Post Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Requested at runtime on Android 13+ when you start a session. Used exclusively to display the silent live countdown notification (with Pause, Resume, and Reset buttons) and the session completion notification when a focus or break phase ends.

Foreground Timer Service FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE

Allows PomodoroTimerService (subtype pomodoro_timer) to keep your countdown running accurately when you switch apps or lock your screen. As soon as you pause or reset the timer, the foreground service stops immediately.

Wake Lock & Backup Wake-up WAKE_LOCK

Holds a temporary partial CPU wake lock while a countdown is actively ticking and for the brief duration of the completion alarm (10–60 seconds) so the device does not sleep through the end of your session. When paused or idle, no wake lock is held.

Vibration & Haptics VIBRATE

Provides subtle tactile feedback on controls and vibrates when a session ends. You can disable alarm vibration at any time inside Alarm Settings.

Internet, Network State & Advertising ID INTERNET · ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE · AD_ID

Used strictly by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK and Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to check consent requirements and load the native ad card. All core timer, alarm, and daily rhythm features work completely offline.

System Ringtone Picker & Audio Ducking ACTION_RINGTONE_PICKER

When you choose a custom alarm sound, Pomodoro delegates selection to Android's built-in system Ringtone Picker so the app never needs permission to read your personal media or audio files. When the alarm rings, it requests transient audio focus (AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN_TRANSIENT_MAY_DUCK) to gently lower study music without stopping it.

RETENTION, DELETION & YOUR RIGHTS

GDPR · UK · CCPA · DPDP

Full control over your data

Resetting Study History

Pomodoro intentionally does not build long-term behavioral profiles. Your completed session count and focused minutes reset automatically at 12:00 AM midnight every day, or immediately whenever you tap RESET TODAY on the Today's Rhythm card.

Complete Erasure

You can permanently erase all local preferences, custom session lengths, alarm settings, and cached consent states at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings → Apps → Pomodoro → Storage & cache → Clear storage, or by uninstalling the app.

EEA, UK & Swiss Users (GDPR): Where the Google Mobile Ads SDK processes device identifiers or IP addresses, legal bases include your explicit consent (collected via the Google UMP consent form) and legitimate interests for basic fraud prevention and security. You may withdraw consent at any time via Alarm Settings → Privacy choices.

California & US State Privacy Laws (CCPA / CPRA / VCDPA): We do not sell your personal information in the traditional sense. To opt out of cross-context behavioral advertising by third-party ad networks, you can manage your preferences in the app's privacy options or enable "Delete advertising ID" / "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in your Android system settings.

Children's Privacy: Pomodoro is a general-audience productivity timer and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
