01 · DEVELOPER IDENTITY, POINT OF CONTACT & SCOPE

Data Controller Information & Local-First Sanctuary Architecture

No User Accounts

This Privacy Policy governs the mobile application Pomodoro (Android package identifier: com.studyapp.pomodoro), developed and published by Nexdert. For any privacy inquiries, data protection requests, or compliance questions, you may contact our Privacy Point of Contact directly at noxedus2008@gmail.com.

Pomodoro is a distraction-free study timer, a 30-day and 365-day rhythm ledger, a subject tracker, and an ambient focus studio for Android. Every feature of the application — including Flow Overtime, the 365-Day Archive, CSV Ledger Export, Custom Study Subjects, Unlimited Daily Tasks, Procedural Ambient Soundscapes, and Focus Mode — is unlocked for all users at no cost.

To fund development without subscriptions or paywalls, Pomodoro uses an ad-supported model powered by Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). We do not operate proprietary backend servers and do not require user accounts.

02 · ON-DEVICE STUDY LEDGER & LOCAL STORAGE

Information Stored Locally on Your Device (Never Shared)

Private Sandbox Only

All study progress, task lists, and personal timer configurations are stored strictly inside your Android device's private application sandbox (pomodoro_timer_state SharedPreferences in Context.MODE_PRIVATE) and are never accessed by us or transmitted to us or any third party:

Daily Rhythm & 365-Day History

Stores daily completed Focus session counts, focused minutes, Flow Overtime minutes, active streak calculations, and your rolling 30-day and 365-day calendar heatmaps. It automatically resets daily counters at 12:00 AM local time while archiving past days on-device for up to 365 days.

Study Subjects & Daily Tasks

Stores your selected study subject tag, any custom subject tags you create (label and color hex), per-subject focused minute allocations, and your daily task checklist (task titles, target Pomodoro counts, completion status, and per-day task archive).

Timer, Alarm & Sound Preferences

Stores custom durations for Focus (1–180 min), Short Break (1–90 min), and Long Break (1–120 min), active countdown timestamps, alarm sound URI, vibration toggle, ring duration (5–120s), and ambient soundscape volume.

User-Initiated CSV Export & Auto Backup

When you tap CSV, the app writes pomodoro_study_ledger_<date>.csv to your device's Downloads folder and opens Android's system share sheet via FileProvider. Android Auto Backup (allowBackup="true") may back up your local preferences to your personal Google Drive if enabled in your device settings.

05 · PROMINENT DISCLOSURE · ANDROID ACCESSIBILITYSERVICE API

Focus Mode App-Exit Blocking (FocusLockAccessibilityService)

Optional Focus Mode

In compliance with Google Play's Use of the AccessibilityService API Policy and the Sensitive Permissions Policy, Pomodoro provides the following explicit disclosure regarding its optional use of Android's AccessibilityService API (com.studyapp.pomodoro.focus.FocusLockAccessibilityService):

1. WHY THE API IS USED & NON-DISABILITY DESIGNATION

Pomodoro is a study productivity timer, not a disability assistance tool, and explicitly declares android:isAccessibilityTool="false" in res/xml/accessibility_focus_lock.xml. The AccessibilityService API is used solely for user-initiated Focus Mode, Auto Focus, and Advanced Focus sessions to prevent accidental distraction by keeping Pomodoro in the foreground while your study timer is active.

2. EXACT EVENTS ACCESSED ON-DEVICE

When you grant consent in the in-app Prominent Disclosure modal and enable Pomodoro in Android Accessibility Settings, the service listens only for window state transitions (TYPE_WINDOW_STATE_CHANGED, TYPE_WINDOWS_CHANGED, TYPE_WINDOW_CONTENT_CHANGED) and navigation key presses (flagRequestFilterKeyEvents for Back, Home, Recents, All Apps, and Notification keys) to collapse the notification shade and bring MainActivity back to the front.

3. ZERO DATA COLLECTION, STORAGE, OR SHARING

The service is configured with android:canRetrieveWindowContent="false" and android:canPerformGestures="false". Pomodoro never reads, inspects, logs, stores, or transmits screen text, UI hierarchy nodes, passwords, personal messages, or keystrokes typed in any application, and zero data from the AccessibilityService API is ever collected or shared with any third party.

4. EMERGENCY, TELECOM & SYSTEM SAFETY EXEMPTIONS

To preserve device safety and platform security controls, FocusLockAccessibilityService automatically exempts incoming/ongoing phone calls, emergency dialers (com.android.incallui, com.google.android.dialer, com.android.phone, com.android.emergency), Android's system permission controller, and active input method keyboards (IMEs). It does not modify system settings or block uninstallation.

07 · DATA RETENTION, DELETION POLICY & USER RIGHTS

How Long Data Is Retained & How to Delete It Immediately

Instant User Control

Data Retention Schedule

  • • On-Device Study History & Tasks: Daily rhythm records and daily task lists are retained locally on your device for a rolling window of up to 365 days (HISTORY_WINDOW_DAYS_YEAR = 365).
  • • No Developer Server Retention: We do not store data on external servers, so our server-side retention period is zero days.
  • • No User Accounts (Google Play Account Deletion Policy): Pomodoro does not support or require account creation, login credentials, or cloud profiles; therefore, no server-side account exists to freeze or delete.
  • • Third-Party Advertising Data: Data processed by Google AdMob is retained and governed by Google's Data Retention Policies.

1. Delete On-Device Study Data

Tap RESET TODAY on the Daily Rhythm card to clear today's session counter, tap × on any custom subject tag or task, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Pomodoro → Storage & cache → Clear storage (or uninstall the app) to permanently erase all 365-day local history.

2. Withdraw Ad Consent (EEA / UK / GDPR)

Open Alarm Settings (top-right alarm icon in Pomodoro) and tap Privacy choices → MANAGE to reopen the Google UMP consent dialog and revoke or customize ad personalization at any time.

3. Reset or Delete Google Advertising ID

On your Android device, open Settings → Google → All services → Ads and select Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID to prevent past ad impressions from being linked across apps.

4. Revoke Accessibility or Notification Access

Toggle Focus Mode off inside the app's Focus pill menu (whenever Advanced Focus is not actively locked in a running timer) or disable Pomodoro's accessibility service anytime under Android Settings → Accessibility → Pomodoro.
